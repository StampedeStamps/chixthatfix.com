Why this name works

Say it once and it sticks. Chicks That Fix rhymes, it names the work (repair, trades, hands-on skill) and it carries an attitude: capable, unbothered, a little cheeky. That is a lot for three words to do, and it does it without a tagline.

It is already a show title, a shop sign, a hashtag, a tool line and a podcast name, with no explaining required. Women-run repair services, women in the trades and women-led DIY channels are no longer a niche story. The name that owns this space should be short, memorable and unmistakably theirs.

Two spellings, one owner. ChicksThatFix.com is the formal name; ChixThatFix.com is the one people type on a phone and the one that fits a social handle. Buying both means nobody else can trade on the shorter spelling, and both resolve wherever you decide.
